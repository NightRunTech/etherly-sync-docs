# Closing a month

When you're done with a month, for example after sending its CSV to your accountant, you can close it in the [document register](/en/guides/document-register). The month's register is saved as it is at that moment and no longer changes, so it stays the same as what your accountant has.

## When you can close

- The month has ended.
- Its credit notes have been fully updated from your invoicing provider, so the register you close is complete. If you see a notice with the **Update credit notes** button, press it and close the month once the notice is gone.
- Closing a month is available on the Agency plan, for the Owner and the Admin.

## How to close a month

<Warning>
Closing can't be undone. Close the month once you've checked it.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the month">
    In the **Document register**, under **Month**, choose the month you want to close.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press “Close month”">
    The **Close month** button is next to **Export CSV**. In the **“Close …?”** dialog, confirm with **Close month**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The month gets the **Closed** badge, and the date it was closed shows above the table. The close is recorded in the **Activity history** with the user who did it.

## Changes after closing

If a document dated in a closed month is added later, or a document of that month is voided or its amounts change, the closed month stays as it was. The change appears in the next open month, under **Corrections to closed months**, labelled **Correction:** with the month it concerns. In the CSV, the **Corrects month** column shows the same month.

For example, if you close September and then use **Add credit note** to record a credit note dated 18 September, you'll find it in October's register as **Correction: September 2026**.

<Note>
In a closed month you can still link a credit note to its original. The credit note's line then says "Linked after the month was closed", otherwise stays as it was when the month closed, and the link adds no correction line.
</Note>

## Why a closed month doesn't change

What you've already given your accountant isn't rewritten. A later change shows as a separate line with the month it corrects, so you both know what changed and when.
